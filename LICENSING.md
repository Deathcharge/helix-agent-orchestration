## Why MPL-2.0

MPL-2.0 is file-level copyleft designed for reusable software libraries:

- anyone may use the library, including in commercial or proprietary products;
- distributed source files containing Samsarix Orchestration code, and
  modifications to those files, remain available under MPL-2.0;
- a larger application may use different terms for its own separate files;
- license and copyright notices in covered source code cannot be stripped; and
- contributors provide the copyright and patent grants stated in the license.

MPL-2.0 does not require user-interface attribution and does not reserve a
commercial-use monopoly. The separate [NOTICE](NOTICE) makes ownership and
contact information easy to preserve, while [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md)
keeps brand rights separate from the source-code license.

## Contributions

Unless a contribution explicitly says otherwise before acceptance, it is
submitted under MPL-2.0, the same license as the project. Samsarix LLC does not
require copyright assignment or a contributor license agreement at this stage.
That keeps contribution terms simple, but it also means Samsarix LLC cannot
unilaterally relicense third-party contributions later.

If future dual licensing becomes a business requirement, obtain legal advice
and adopt an appropriate contributor agreement before accepting contributions
